# Daira-e-Hunar (دائرۂ ہنر) — AI Service Orchestrator for Pakistan's Informal Economy

> Har shehr ka apna daira, har banday ka apna hunar.
> ہر شہر کا اپنا دائرہ، ہر بندے کا اپنا ہنر۔
> (Every city its own circle, every person their own skill.)

---

## Project Overview

Daira-e-Hunar (meaning "The Circle of Skill") is an agentic AI system designed to understand multilingual service requests (English, Urdu, Roman Urdu), find the best-fit provider using LLM-driven reasoning, and book appointments.

Our vision is centered on **provider dignity, leveling economic reach, and cultural unity**. By bringing the informal economy into a structured, accessible circle, we connect working-class neighborhoods across 8 Pakistani cities to the digital marketplace — all with full transparency via structured trace logs.

## Privacy Note

To uphold the dignity and privacy of informal workers, **all provider data in this project is synthetic** and labeled with `_synthetic: true`. We built with realistic mock data, authentic regional names, and accurate working-class neighborhood coordinates to avoid using anyone's real identity without their explicit consent.
